Poets and authors

Writers from around the metro area, published in full rather than teased. Every piece here appears with its author’s permission and remains entirely theirs.

How work gets here

Submissions are open to anyone writing in the metro area and are read by a person. The grant a writer agrees to is non-exclusive, revocable and credited: they keep copyright, they can publish the same piece anywhere else, and one email takes it down. The terms are short enough to read in a minute.
